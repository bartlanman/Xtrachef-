# Weekly routine (Monday, ~45 min)

## Prices & vendors
- **Contracted price variance** and **Price tracker** (Close Watch items) — catch Sysco/PFG price creep. `kb/reporting/price-tracker.md`, `kb/reporting/cost-management-reports.md`
- Approval rule **Contract Price Variance** (gear > Approval rules, Tenant Admin) flags over-contract prices automatically. `kb/invoicing/approvals.md`
- **Vendor price comparison** feeds the `pfg-sysco-price-check` skill.

## Food & beverage cost
- **Food cost weekly analytics** and **Category weekly analytics**. `kb/reporting/food-cost-weekly-analytics.md`, `kb/reporting/category-weekly-analytics.md`
- **Spending by vendor / category / GL** for anything unusual. `kb/reporting/spending-by-*.md`

## Inventory (Pro)
- Count on schedule; counts are end-of-day — a morning count is scheduled for the previous business day. `kb/count-lists/inventory-counts.md`
- Completed count export (Excel/PDF) includes **Need to Order** — use it as the order sheet.
- Pars are per count list, in purchase units, can vary by month.

## Menu & recipes
- New/changed Toast menu items → map to recipes (Product Mix isn't retroactive). **Re-sync from POS** pulls published changes immediately. `kb/recipes/product-mix-mapping.md`, `kb/getting-started/toast-menu-considerations.md`
- Recipes cost at the latest invoice price — one bad invoice line skews every recipe using it. Review recipe cost changes.

## Housekeeping
- Archive the week's rejected invoices so the rejection count stays meaningful.
- New vendor? Set extract preference (Purchase Detail for food/bev/brewing; Purchase Summary for utilities/repairs/linen) and confirm its vendor code matches QBO "Display Name As". `kb/vendors/`
