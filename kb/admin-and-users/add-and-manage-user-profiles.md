## What it is
User profiles (logins) are **created in Toast Web** and sync into xtraCHEF. Inside xtraCHEF, a Tenant Admin only assigns location access and a role, resets passwords, edits details and revokes access.

## When you'd use it
- A new manager or bookkeeper needs access.
- Someone is locked out.
- An employee leaves.

## How to do it
1. **Create the person** in Toast Web (see "Log in to Toast: Create an Account").
2. In xtraCHEF, go to **gear icon > Users** and search for the person.
3. **Reset password**: click the **key icon**, then **Yes**. The user gets a reset email.
4. **Edit**: click the **pencil icon** to change name, location or role. Location and role are on the **User Access** tab.
5. **Revoke access**: untick the checkbox on the blue bar showing your tenant name.

## Settings, options & permissions
- **Tenant Admin only** can manage profiles and assign roles.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **Email addresses can't be changed** by the user or the admin. Contact Customer Care or your Onboarding Consultant.
- Because logins come from Toast Web, removing someone from Toast Web is part of offboarding too. Revoking xtraCHEF access alone isn't enough.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Add "revoke xtraCHEF tenant access" to the offboarding checklist alongside the Toast Web removal.
- Review the Users list once a quarter for former staff and anyone holding more than one role.
- Use shared role patterns: kitchen lead = Restaurant User, GM = Restaurant Manager, bookkeeper = Tenant Admin or Executive (with care), line staff who photograph invoices = Invoice Capture.
