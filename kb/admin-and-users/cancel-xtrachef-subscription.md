## What it is
This is a short how-to for removing xtraCHEF (including an Essentials trial) from a Toast account. Cancellation happens in Toast Web's subscription management, not inside xtraCHEF.

## When you'd use it
- Ending an xtraCHEF Essentials trial.
- Downgrading or removing xtraCHEF from your Toast products.

## How to do it
1. Sign in to **Toast Web**.
2. Go to **Toast account > My products > Subscriptions**.
3. Find xtraCHEF and manage or cancel it from there.
4. If there's no Subscriptions page, or xtraCHEF isn't listed, start a **live chat** with Customer Support.

## Settings, options & permissions
- You need access to the Toast account's Subscriptions page. The article doesn't name a role, but in practice this is the account owner or admin.
- **Toast Retail** customers see the Subscriptions page **view-only** and must contact Customer Care to make changes.
- Customers who have a Retail *module* can still self-serve.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Live chat support for xtraCHEF runs **Mon–Fri, 9:00 a.m.–5:30 p.m. ET**. You can reach it from Toast Web, Toast Payroll and Toast Now.
- The article doesn't cover billing proration, refunds, data retention or what happens to synced QBO data.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Before cancelling, export what you need: reports (CSV), item library or product lists, recipe costs and inventory counts. Also make sure every invoice has been extracted to QBO (check Extract Monitor) so the books aren't missing bills.
- Turn off **Sales sync** in Sales Setup first so no half-mapped JEs post during the wind-down.
- For a trial, put the trial end date on a calendar so the decision is deliberate.
