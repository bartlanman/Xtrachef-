## What it is
The xtraCHEF mobile app (iOS App Store / Google Play) shows menus, recipes and prep recipes as **view-only** cards. Each card has prep and cook times, serving info, ingredients, and preparation & procedures.

## When you'd use it
- On the line or at the prep station, as a digital recipe book that matches the costed spec.
- To train new cooks or bartenders on specs.

## How to do it
1. Install the xtraCHEF app and log in.
2. Tap **More** in the bottom nav, then **Recipe**.
3. You land on **Menu**. Pick a menu to see its recipes.
4. Or switch to the **Recipes** or **Prep Recipes** tab.
5. Tap a recipe and scroll to the tabs for ingredients and preparation & procedures.

## Settings, options & permissions
- The article lists no roles beyond having an app login.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- The article only describes viewing. Recipe creation and editing happen on desktop.
- What appears depends on the Procedures data (instructions, images, allergens) entered on desktop.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Fill in **Instructions**, **Recipe image**, allergens and **Tools** for every recipe on desktop. The app then becomes the line's spec book with no extra laminated cards to maintain.
- Bar specs: put cocktail builds and draft pour specs (glassware, pour size) in Procedures so bartenders follow the same spec the cost is based on.
