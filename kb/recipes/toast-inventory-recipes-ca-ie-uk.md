## What it is
Recipe guide for **Toast Inventory** (not xtraCHEF), which is in Beta in Canada, Ireland and the UK. It applies only to customers who bought Toast Inventory before July 2026. Later buyers are pointed to **Toast IQ Inventory**. **It doesn't apply to Basic City (US / xtraCHEF).** It's kept here for completeness and for comparison.

## When you'd use it
- Only for a non-US Toast Inventory account. It's also useful for understanding how Toast's native inventory differs from xtraCHEF.

## How to do it
1. **Inventory > Recipes** → **New Recipe** → choose **Recipes** or **Prep Recipes**.
2. *Basic details* for a recipe: **Recipe name** (pencil icon), **Location** (read-only), **Menu item** (POS link), **Notes**. A prep recipe has **Batch size** in place of a menu item, and shows how many recipes use it.
3. *Ingredients:* **Add Ingredient**, then **Qty** and **UOM**. **Total Cost** fills in.
4. *Allergens:* "This recipe contains" and "This recipe may contain traces of". These are pulled from ingredients and can be edited, and they don't affect cost.
5. **Save changes** publishes. **Draft** saves without affecting reports.

## Settings, options & permissions
- There are **no permission levels**. Anyone can see and edit recipes.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- An ingredient can have only **two UOMs**, and you can only pick a UOM compatible with its setup.
- Every ingredient row must be complete before **Save** is enabled.
- Draft costs are left out of COGS reporting.
- A prep recipe becomes a stock item in the Ingredient Library. Its secondary UOM has to be added there.
- **No in-recipe yield.** The workaround is a prep recipe (e.g., 1000 g onion in, 900 g batch out).
- Recipes can't be built for modifiers yet. Only menu items and prep items are supported.
- PMIX shows **current** recipe cost regardless of the date range.
- Allergens aren't shown to guests yet. Allergen data is a limited release.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Not applicable to Basic City. The contrast is useful, though: xtraCHEF (US) **does** support in-recipe yield, modifier mapping with Subtract, and size mapping, all of which this product lacks.
