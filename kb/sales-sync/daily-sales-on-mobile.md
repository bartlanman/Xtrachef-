## What it is
The xtraCHEF mobile app (iOS and Android) has a **Daily Sales** page. It shows Toast-synced sales for each location and day, broken out by COGS group. From there you can make manual +/- adjustments and enter closing inventory by COGS group, which posts to the Sync Monitor. It's a mobile version of part of the Sync Monitor.

## When you'd use it
- To check a day's sales by COGS group away from a desk.
- To enter month-end closing inventory values on the floor.

## How to do it
1. Install the **xtraCHEF** app (App Store or Google Play) and log in with your Toast credentials. If you have several tenants, pick one.
2. Tap **More** in the nav bar, then **Daily Sales**.
3. The first location and today's date show by default. Change **Location** or **Date** on the calendar as needed.
4. Read **Total Sales ($)** (from the POS) and **Total Adjustment** (manual changes), with the breakdown by COGS group below.
5. **Adjust**: tap the **+/- Adjustment** icon, edit, then tap **Done**. It updates immediately.
6. **Closing inventory**: on **Edit Sales**, tap **Add Closing Inventory**, enter values per COGS group, then tap **Post to Sync Monitor** and **Done**. On desktop, filter the Sync Monitor calendar by **Inventory** or **Closing inventory** to see them.

## Settings, options & permissions
- The article doesn't specify roles. The equivalent Sync Monitor access is TA, EX, RM and RU.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Manual adjustments change xtraCHEF's sales figures, and through the Sync Monitor they can affect what posts. Use them sparingly and check **View Audit**.
- Enter closing inventory on the **last day of the accounting period**, or COGS ignores it.
- Pro users can use the **Inventory by COGS** report instead of typing values in.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- On Essentials (no inventory module), this is the quick way to feed month-end closing inventory per COGS group (Food, Beer, Liquor, NA Bev) into the COGS report. Do it on the last night of the month, right after the physical count.
- The GM can check the daily COGS-group sales on their phone each morning as a first sign of sync problems.
