## What it is
**gear icon > Locations** lists every location on the xtraCHEF tenant with its name, creation date, account number, and the location's **email-in address**. From here you view or edit the address, shipping address, location type or concept, payment method, and, for QBO-at-location-level setups, the QuickBooks link details.

## When you'd use it
- To find a location's invoice email address.
- To update a shipping address (which order POs use) or the payment method.
- To verify the QuickBooks location mapping when GL import or the Integrations tab is missing.

## How to do it
1. Go to **gear icon > Locations** (upper right).
2. Select a location first. The buttons stay disabled until you do.
3. **View Location Details** shows the physical address, shipping address, and category.
4. **Edit Location Details** opens the same form, editable:
   - Change **Location Type** to match the current concept.
   - The **Configuration** tab has the **payment method** and **shipping address**.
   - The email-in prefix (**Email invoices to**, before @xcinvoice.com) is also edited here (see the Upload Desktop article).
5. Click **Save**. Changes apply immediately.

## Settings, options & permissions
- No roles are stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Emailing a document to the location address uploads it without signing in, but only from registered users and only as PDFs (per the upload article).
- Changing the email prefix stops the old address from working.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Record each location's xcinvoice address in the team's vendor onboarding checklist and in vendor contact records.
- Check the shipping address here before using scheduled order guides, because it feeds the **Ship To** on POs.
