## What it is (2-4 sentences)
The Price Tracker shows price changes on invoice items you buy, with average % change and dollar impact. It starts capturing within the first few uploads and is available as a dashboard tile, a full page, and a cost management report.

## When you'd use it
- Spotting vendor price increases, choosing substitutes, justifying vendor switches, triggering menu price reviews.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. Dashboard tile shows recent shifts, **Average price change percentage** and **$ impact**.
2. Click **Price Tracker** at the top of the tile for the full page.
3. Filters (any/none; updates instantly): **Location**, **Vendor**, **Category** (from Map Invoice Items/Item Review), **Filter Close Watch Items** toggle (items marked close watch in Item Library), **Sort by** (positive→negative or reverse), **Period 1 / Period 2** (this vs last week, last vs prior week, last 4 vs prior 4 weeks); also a fluctuation-range slider.
4. Table per invoice item with history line chart: **Min price**, **Max price**, **Average price change**, **Total quantity purchased value**, **$ impact**.
5. **v caret** on a row expands detail.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Compares invoice items (not products), so a vendor item-code change can look like a new item.
- Comparison windows are fixed presets.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Mark key cost drivers (hops, malt, CO2, proteins, fryer oil, cans) as **Close Watch** and check that filter weekly, sorted positive→negative.
- Any item with large $ impact → re-run recipe costing / Variance Analysis and consider menu price changes.
