## What it is (2-4 sentences)
An Analytics report showing spend by category week by week, with a month pie chart and drill-down to day, vendor and invoice line. Three views: Summary, Week, Invoice Item Details.

## When you'd use it
- Tracking how category spend trends week to week; finding the highest-ticket items; catching a spike in real time.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. **Analytics** → **Category Weekly** tab.
2. Summary (MTD by category) loads for current month/week. Use the **calendar icon** (top right) for other ranges → **Refresh**. Slider: **Period to Date** / **Month to Date** (default).
3. Pie chart = month spend by category (updates weekly); table below = weekly spend by category.
4. Click the **week header** → Week view (per-day cost by category and vendor).
5. Click a **date** or **vendor** → Invoice Item Details; **View** icon opens the invoice.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Data only reflects processed invoices; weeks with unprocessed uploads look low.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Good Monday-morning glance; drill into any category whose week is materially above its run rate.
