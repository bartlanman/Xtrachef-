## What it is (2-4 sentences)
A bar chart of spending per active xtraCHEF category, with each bar split by location. Useful for seeing where purchase dollars go at a level below GL codes (e.g., Hops vs Malt vs Produce).

## When you'd use it
- Quick weekly view of category spend; spotting a category that jumped.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. **Reporting** → **Spending by Category** (also under Analytics).
2. Default: all locations, most recent calendar week.
3. Change location/date range → **Refresh**.
4. Each bar shows location contributions; total for the range at the far right; hover for per-location breakdown.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated. Better with Custom Categories set up.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Only active categories show; uncategorized items don't contribute.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Design categories to match how you manage (brewing raw materials, packaging, kitchen food, bar NA, supplies) so this chart answers questions directly.
