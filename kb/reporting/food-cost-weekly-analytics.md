## What it is (2-4 sentences)
An Analytics report showing food cost (sales vs purchases by **COGS group**) for a month, with drill-down by week, vendor and invoice line. It ties Toast sales to invoice purchases so you can see each COGS group's cost percentage. Requires COGS mapping.

## When you'd use it
- Weekly cost-of-goods check (food, beer ingredients, NA beverage, supplies) mid-month instead of waiting for month-end.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. **Analytics > Food Cost Weekly**.
2. Summary view loads for current month; choose location(s) and/or month(s) → **Refresh**.
3. Toggle **Month to Date** / **Period to Date** (auto-updates).
4. Read three charts: **Sales by COGS**, **Cost Breakdown**, **Purchase by COGS** (hover for $ / %; click a key entry to hide/show a COGS group).
5. Click a **Cost Breakdown** bar → **Weekly** view for that group (weeks + Month to Date column; % of cost). Click a week → vendors and daily purchases. **Back to Summary** to return.
6. Click a vendor → **Invoice Item Details**; **+** in **View** column opens the original invoice.
7. Export: **Download PDF** (summary, next to the toggle); **Excel icon** on Summary/Weekly/Invoice Item tables.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- Prereq: **COGS mapping** complete. No role limits stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Unmapped sales categories or purchase categories drop out of the COGS view — accuracy depends on mapping.
- Purchases only include uploaded/processed invoices; missing invoices understate cost.
- Period to Date follows your xtraCHEF accounting period setup.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Every Monday: export the Weekly table to Excel and compare beer/food COGS % to targets; flag any group >2 pts over target.
- PDF summary is a ready-made weekly email attachment for managers.
