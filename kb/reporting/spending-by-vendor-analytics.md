## What it is (2-4 sentences)
A pie chart + table of spend by vendor that drills down vendor → GL → category → invoice item → individual invoices. It shows who you spend the most with and what drives that spend.

## When you'd use it
- Vendor negotiations, finding which items drive a vendor's total, checking a vendor's price creep.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. **Reporting** (left nav) → **Cost visualization** → **Spending by vendor**.
2. Default: first location, current week. Change location/date → **Refresh** (upper right).
3. **Vendor view**: remove vendors from the **Vendor List** (or **Deselect all** then add back); **Amount Section Slider** limits by total.
4. Click a vendor → **GL view** (filter GLs, price range).
5. Click a GL → **Categories view** (needs custom categories).
6. Click a category → **Invoice Item view**.
7. Click an item → **Invoice view**: timeline (hover totals, click point opens scan); **View icon** opens original invoice.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Categories level is empty without custom categories.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Monthly: export top-10 vendors and compare to QBO "Expenses by Vendor Summary" for the same dates — mismatches reveal unextracted invoices or bills entered directly in QBO.
- Feed the PFG vs Sysco price-check routine with the Invoice Item view for shared items.
