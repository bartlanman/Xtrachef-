## What it is
A catalog of everything on the Reporting tab, grouped into Cost Management, Cost Visualization, Inventory and Other. Data comes from uploaded invoices and synced Toast sales; reports can be viewed in xtraCHEF or downloaded.

## When you'd use it
- Choosing the right report and checking its prerequisites first.

## How to do it
1. Open **Reporting** in the left nav. Use the search bar to find a report; click the **star** to add it to **Favorites**.
2. Report groups:
   - **Cost management**: Contracted price variance; Cost of goods sold (COGS); Operating summary (by week, period or quarter); Price fluctuation comparison; Price tracker; Purchased items; Vendor price comparison.
   - **Cost visualization** (also under Analytics): Spending by category, Spending by GL, Spending by vendor, Unit comparison.
   - **Inventory**: Actual vs theoretical, Depleting inventory, Inventory by COGS, Inventory by GL, Inventory summary, Inventory variance.
   - **Other**: Variance analysis (also under Recipe); **Invoice summary by GL** (every reconciled invoice in a date range with a collapsible list of its GL codes — replaces the retired GL distribution and GL summary reports); **Sales reporting** (summary of sales synced from Toast Web; select rows with radio buttons or all with the top-left checkbox to download).
3. Inventory report prerequisites:
   1. Upload at least 20 invoices.
   2. Create products.
   3. Map all invoice items to GL codes/categories.
   4. Complete COGS mapping.
   5. Set pack/size/UOM for each item.
   6. Complete area setup and at least one count list.
   7. Complete two inventory counts.

## Settings, options & permissions
- None stated in the article.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- GL distribution and GL summary reports are retired; use Invoice summary by GL.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Invoice summary by GL is the best report for tying out QBO bills by vendor and GL for the same period.
- Star the weekly reports so Favorites becomes the routine.
