## What it is (2-4 sentences)
The second section of the Reporting page: four expense-focused reports — Spending by Category, Spending by GL (formerly Spend Summary by GL), Spending by Vendor, and Unit Comparison. GL distribution and GL summary reports were replaced by **Invoice summary by GL**.

## When you'd use it
- Comparing and tracking expenses against total spend.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. **Reporting** → scroll to cost visualization; **star** to favorite.
2. **Spending by Category** — by location(s) and date range.
3. **Spending by GL** — date range → **Generate Report** → purchases and credits per location → **downward arrow** in **View** column → all GLs with totals → **View** on a GL row for category breakdown.
4. **Spending by Vendor** — vendors, then their GLs, categories, invoice items.
5. **Unit Comparison** — compare locations.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Spending by GL shows credits separately — net them when comparing to QBO.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Spending by GL purchases minus credits by GL = the number to tie to QBO expense accounts for bills from xtraCHEF.
