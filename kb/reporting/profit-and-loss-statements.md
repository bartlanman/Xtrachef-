## What it is (2-4 sentences)
A short FAQ answering whether Toast has a P&L report: it does not. The closest equivalents are xtraCHEF's **Operating Summary**, GL reporting in Toast Web / Toast Payroll / xtraCHEF, a third-party tool like Restaurant365, or your accounting system.

## When you'd use it
- When someone asks for a P&L from Toast — point them to QBO for the official P&L and the Operating Summary for the operational view.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. Official P&L: run it in QuickBooks Online.
2. Operational P&L: xtraCHEF **Operating Summary** — compare with the QBO P&L to find discrepancies in either system.
3. GL tools: Toast Web GL code mapping, Toast Payroll static GL allocations, xtraCHEF GL codes.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- N/A.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- The article states that Toast Payroll's direct QBO integration only syncs payroll JE data automatically (sales/AP Toast Web syncs are Retail-only/limited release per other articles; xtraCHEF handles sales JEs and bills for restaurants).

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Month-end ritual: Operating Summary vs QBO P&L side by side; investigate any line differing by more than a set threshold.
