## What it is (2-4 sentences)
The dashboard is xtraCHEF's landing page with quick-view tiles: an onboarding progress bar, Invoice status, Price Tracker and Spend. It's the fastest daily health check.

## When you'd use it
- Every login: see invoices needing action, price jumps, and week spend.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
- **Progress bar**: disappears after **10 invoices** are processed and extracted (recommendation: upload at least 10).
- **Date range** calendar dropdown: **This week**, **Last week**, **Last 4 weeks**.
- **Invoice tile**: counts extracted / processing / rejected-or-action-needed; upload entry point; **caret (>)** opens **Invoices** page to check status or approve.
- **Price Tracker tile**: top item fluctuations week over week (needs ≥2 weeks of data); pick comparison period in top bar; **caret** opens full Price Tracker.
- **Spend tile**: invoice total for the period; with COGS groups mapped to sales and purchase categories it shows $ and % by spending category; **caret** opens **Food Cost Ratio** page.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Price Tracker tile empty until 2 weeks of data.
- Spend breakdown needs COGS mapping.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Daily: clear the Invoice tile's action-needed count to zero — that's what blocks QBO extracts.
