## What it is (2-4 sentences)
Overview of the **Analytics** tab, which auto-builds visual quick-glance reports from purchase and sales data. Lists Spending by GL, Category Weekly, Food Cost Weekly, Spending by Category, Spending by Vendor, Volume and Unit Comparison.

## When you'd use it
- Orienting to which analytic answers which question.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
1. Click **Analytics** in the nav bar.
2. Choose a report:
   - **Spending by GL** — spend by GL for a date range; drill into sub-categories.
   - **Category Weekly** — week-to-week side-by-side category spend.
   - **Food Cost Weekly** — sales vs spend by COGS group (requires **COGS Mapping**).
   - **Spending by Category** — like GL but by category.
   - **Spending by Vendor** — top vendors and their GLs/categories/items.
   - **Volume** — listed, not described.
   - **Unit Comparison** — compare locations (multi-location).

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- None stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Food Cost Weekly is empty without COGS mapping.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Analytics tables export to Excel — use them as weekly data feeds.
