## What it is (2-4 sentences)
Introduces xtraCHEF's two insight areas: **Reporting** (historical tables, CSV export) and **Analytics** (visual drill-down charts, Excel export), plus Budget, Recipe reporting and Inventory Analytics (the latter two are xtraCHEF Pro).

## When you'd use it
- Getting oriented after onboarding; deciding which tool answers a question.

## How to do it (numbered steps with exact menu paths, button and field names)
**Prereqs for useful data**: upload invoices/receipts; clear action-needed invoices (Invoice Exceptions); map invoice items to products. This also makes invoices extract-ready for QBO.
- **Reporting** (left nav): six categories — Cost management, Cost visualization, Recipe, Inventory, Accounting, Sales. Tables of history; **export to CSV**.
- **Analytics** (left nav): defaults to **Spending by GL**; drill tabs **GL Code**, **Category**, **Invoice Item**, **Invoices**; tables download to **Excel**.
- **Budget**: forecast sales and budget; compare actual vs estimated COGS during the month.
- **Recipe reporting** (Pro): Variance Analysis, Product Mix Report.
- **Inventory Analytics** (Pro): Actual vs. Theoretical Analysis, Depleting Inventory.

## Settings, options & permissions (which roles can do it)
- Recipe and Inventory features require **xtraCHEF Pro**.

## Rules, limits & gotchas (sync errors, mapping rules, what syncs vs what doesn't, timing)
- Upload all spend (food vendors, utilities, equipment, services) so reports cover the whole business.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- CSV/Excel exports are the bridge for agent automation: pull weekly exports and compare against QBO data.
