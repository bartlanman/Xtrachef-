## What it is
A short Q&A on which Toast product should own which kind of inventory. xtraCHEF is the AP-automation and food-cost tool. It tracks inventory at the ingredient level and ties ingredients to Toast menu items. Toast Retail is for SKU-heavy retail selling: retail stock counts, barcodes, and stock levels pushed to online and third-party channels.

## When you'd use it
- When deciding where merch, packaged beer to-go (cans, crowlers, 4-packs) and retail goods should be tracked, as opposed to kitchen and bar ingredients.
- When a hybrid restaurant/retail operation is considering both products.

## How to do it
No procedure. The decision guidance is:
1. Prepared food and drinks, tracked at the ingredient level and mapped to menu items: use **xtraCHEF**.
2. Significant retail operations (many SKUs, barcode generation, stock that has to stay accurate across in-house ordering, Toast Online Ordering, third-party delivery and ecommerce such as Shopify): use **Toast Retail**, where quantities live in the POS.
3. Businesses that do both can use both products.

## Settings, options & permissions
None listed.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **xtraCHEF ingredient quantities stay inside xtraCHEF.** They are not shared with the POS or any other system. xtraCHEF will not 86 items or update online-ordering stock.
- **xtraCHEF is US-only.** It is not available in Australia, Canada, Ireland or the UK, where core Toast Inventory works differently.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- For Basic City, track draft beer, kitchen ingredients and bar inputs in xtraCHEF. If the retail side grows (merch, packaged beer sold online or through Shopify-style channels), Toast Retail is the tool that keeps sellable stock in sync across channels. Don't expect xtraCHEF counts to drive POS stock-outs.
- If retail stays small, packaged beer can still be counted in xtraCHEF as Case/Pack/Can items (see the Item Library note) so the cost gets captured.
