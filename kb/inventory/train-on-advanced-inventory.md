## What it is
A training hub page, not a how-to. It points to a three-part short video course on getting value out of inventory, COGS and prime-cost reporting in xtraCHEF: how counts work, how food cost is tracked, and how recipes feed actual-vs-theoretical (AvT) reporting. It also lists the reading that goes with each video.

## When you'd use it
- After onboarding, when you're ready to move your paper or spreadsheet counts into xtraCHEF.
- To train a new kitchen manager, bar manager or GM on the inventory-to-COGS workflow.
- As a checklist of which articles to read, in order, for inventory, COGS and recipes.

## How to do it
1. Finish the recommended prerequisites first:
   1. Set up and configure the xtraCHEF account.
   2. Meet your xtraCHEF Onboarding Consultant and complete Onboarding.
   3. Attend the xtraCHEF Expert-Led Webinar Series (https://central.toasttab.com/s/article/xtraCHEF-Expert-Led-Training-Sessions).
2. Watch the three videos, about 3 minutes each:
   - **Inventory 202: Inventory Management**: taking inventory, inventory fluctuation, inventory reports. https://play.vidyard.com/3GKRtKp9Gjyw46RPmGa6ww
   - **Inventory 303: Food Costs**: food cost, COGS, cost tracking, vendor monitoring. https://play.vidyard.com/uugC6LY1hrAY6wZpWLnaoh
   - **Inventory 404: Product and Menu Management**: recipe costing strategy and actual vs. theoretical reporting. https://play.vidyard.com/kQQPDdD11Zh9mT4odMCDXo
3. After each video, read its companion articles:
   - 202 goes with Area Setup & Count Lists, Inventory Counts, Inventory Analytics and Mobile Inventory.
   - 303 goes with Accounting Periods, Add/Edit COGS Groups, COGS Mapping and COGS Report.
   - 404 goes with Get Started With Recipes, Recipe Product Mix Mapping, Variance Analysis & Product Mix Reports and Inventory Analytics.

## Settings, options & permissions
None listed. This is an index page.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- The course order matters. Counts come first, then COGS groups and mapping, then recipes and PMIX mapping. AvT only works once all three layers exist.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Use the 202 → 303 → 404 order as the onboarding roadmap for Basic City: (1) areas and count lists for kitchen, bar, cold room/keg cooler and dry storage; (2) COGS groups split into Food, Beer (house), Beer (guest/purchased), Wine, Liquor, NA Bev and Packaging/Disposables; (3) recipes for food plus pour recipes for draft by ounce.
- Make the three videos required watching for every manager who counts. Together they take under 10 minutes.
