## What it is
**Order History** is the searchable log of purchase orders placed through xtraCHEF's Order module. You can filter by status, vendor, location, and a date range. From an order you can download it as a PDF, email a copy, or convert a fulfilled order into an invoice.

## When you'd use it
- To check what was ordered against what was delivered and invoiced.
- To resend a PO to a rep, or pull a PO for a dispute.
- To find a past order you want to repeat.

## How to do it
1. Click **Order** in the nav menu.
2. Click **Order History**.
3. Set the filters. **Status**, **Vendor**, and **Location** are optional. **From** and **To** dates are required; use the calendar icons to pick them.
4. Click **Submit**.
5. Review the results. Click an order to see its full details, where you can **download a PDF** of the PO or **email** a copy to any address.
6. For a fulfilled order you can click **Convert to Invoice**. See the gotcha below.

## Settings, options & permissions
- The article states no roles or plan tier.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **Avoid Convert to Invoice for accounting.** The vendor's real invoice reflects substitutions and price changes. A converted PO won't, so costing, AP, and reports go wrong. Upload the vendor's actual invoice instead.
- The date range is mandatory. If you get no results, check From/To first.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Weekly check: compare Order History, for orders that are fulfilled but not invoiced, against Invoices > Completed. A missing invoice means a missing bill in QBO.
- Use the email-copy feature to cc the receiver or kitchen manager, so they know what to expect at delivery.
