## What it is
The **bell icon** in the web app lists recent **Invoice Exception** notifications, meaning invoices flagged Action needed and paused from reconciling. Click one to go straight to the invoice and fix it. (The article sits in the Orders collection but is about invoices.)

## When you'd use it
- For a quick triage of new exceptions without opening the Invoices page.

## How to do it
1. Click the **bell icon** (upper right) to open recent flagged invoices.
2. Click a notification to open that invoice.
3. Fix the underlying problem. xtraCHEF sends the invoice back into processing automatically.
4. Housekeeping: hover over a notification. The **trash can** deletes it and the **envelope** marks it read.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Only one type is described: **Invoice Exception**. No settings or roles are listed.
- Push notifications on mobile are configured separately (see Mobile Notifications).

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Deleting a notification doesn't fix the invoice. The invoice stays in Action needed.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Treat the bell as an inbox to empty every day. Resolve, then mark read, so a backlog stands out.
- For full coverage use **Invoices > Action needed**, because the bell only shows recent items.
