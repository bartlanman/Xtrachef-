## What it is
The xtraCHEF mobile app can build and send vendor orders away from a desk. You can order from vendors' past invoice items or from order guides built on desktop. The cart shows an estimated total based on recent invoice prices.

## When you'd use it
- Walk-in or storeroom ordering: build the order while you look at the shelves.

## How to do it
1. Install the xtraCHEF app (iOS or Android) and log in.
2. Tap **More**, then **Order**, and confirm the location.
3. Tap **New Order** (or **Order History** to see past orders).
4. Choose **Vendors** (build from scratch) or an existing **Order Guide**.
5. With Vendors: pick the supplier, then items. Use the **Quantity** drop-down and tap **Add**.
6. Tap the **cart icon** (top right). Set the **Delivery Date**, add **Notes** for the vendor, and check the ship-to address, invoice-to address, and sales rep details.
7. Review the estimated total (based on your most recent invoice prices).
8. Tap **Place Order** and save it as a PDF or email it.

## Settings, options & permissions
- No roles or tier are stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Order guides have to be **created on desktop** first.
- **Internal orders (central kitchen) aren't available on mobile.** The article also says Order Guides aren't supported in the mobile Order module, which contradicts the step that lets you use an existing guide. Expect partial support: you can use desktop-built guides but not manage them.
- Totals are estimates from the last invoice price.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Pair with par-based order guides so a manager can walk the storeroom and fill quantities quickly.
- Add vendor **Notes** for substitutions or delivery-window requests.
