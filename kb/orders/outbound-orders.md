## What it is
The **Order** landing page has a **Recent Orders** log of placed and in-progress orders, plus an **Ordering by Vendor** pie chart. Each order shows who placed it and whether the vendor has confirmed it.

## When you'd use it
- To check that a rep confirmed an order before the delivery window gets tight.
- To see who ordered what, and which vendors take most of your spend.

## How to do it
1. Click **Order** in the nav menu.
2. **Recent Orders** (left) lists orders sent by **Download PDF** or **via Email**. Each entry has the vendor, order number, order date, deliver-by date, the team member who placed it, and other details.
3. Check the **Status** column for vendor confirmation. It updates automatically when the vendor clicks **Confirm Order** in the automated email xtraCHEF sends with an emailed order.
4. Click the blue **email** link at the bottom of an entry to see the recipient's address.
5. **Ordering by Vendor** pie chart: recent outbound order dollars and percentage by vendor.

## Settings, options & permissions
- No roles are stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Confirmation tracking only works for orders **sent by email** from xtraCHEF. PDF orders have no automatic confirmation.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Send orders by email so you get confirmation status. An unconfirmed order a day before delivery means call the rep.
- Use the pie chart for quarterly vendor-concentration reviews and to prepare for price negotiations.
