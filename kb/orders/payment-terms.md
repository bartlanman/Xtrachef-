## What it is
**Payment terms** (for example, Net 30 or due on receipt) define when vendor invoices are due. You create them in xtraCHEF or import them from QuickBooks Online, then assign a default term to each vendor in the Vendor Hub. They should match your actual supplier agreements.

## When you'd use it
- During initial setup, so due dates on bills are right.
- When a vendor's terms change.

## How to do it
All of this is under **gear icon > Payment terms**.

**Import from QBO** (only when QBO is integrated; otherwise the button doesn't appear)
1. Click **Import Payment Terms from QuickBooks Online** at the top.
2. Click **Import** in the pop-up. The terms are available in the Vendor Hub right away.

**Add**
1. Click **+Add Payment Terms**.
2. Name the term and define how it calculates.
3. Under **Let's test the due date for the term defined**, click **Calculate** to check it.
4. Click **Save**.

**Edit / View**
1. Click a term so it's highlighted blue.
2. Click **Edit Payment Terms** (the same pop-up), change it, and click **Save**. It applies immediately. Or click **View Payment Terms** to see it without changing anything.

**Assign to a vendor**: in the Vendor Hub, use **Select Payment Terms** (the default payment terms drop-down) and pick the term.

## Settings, options & permissions
- QBO integration is required for the import. No roles are stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- The import is a one-time pull. The article doesn't describe continuous sync, so re-import after you add terms in QBO. *(Inference.)*
- The article doesn't explain how terms flow to QBO bill due dates.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Import from QBO first instead of retyping, so the names match exactly in both systems.
- Set a default term on every vendor during onboarding. Correct due dates feed AP aging and cash planning.
