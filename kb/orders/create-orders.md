## What it is
The **Order** module lets you build purchase orders from items you've bought before, either from the full **Catalog** of past invoice items or from an **Order Guide**. You then send the PO to the vendor's sales rep as a PDF or as an email from xtraCHEF. One cart can hold several vendors.

## When you'd use it
- Placing weekly food, beverage, or supply orders with reps who take emailed POs.
- Standardizing what staff order (brand, pack size) by ordering only from approved items.

## How to do it
**Build the cart**
1. Click **Order** in the left nav. The page shows recent orders and **Ordering by Vendor** analytics.
2. Click the green **Order** button.
3. Choose **Catalog** view or **Order Guide** view.
4. Pick the **vendor**.
5. Pick the **Invoice Item** and enter the **Quantity**.
6. Click the **blue +** to add it to the cart.
7. Repeat for every item and vendor, then click **Checkout**.

**Place the order**
1. Fill in the purchase and delivery details. Do this for each vendor in the cart.
2. **Order Date** defaults to today. Change it or the **Deliver By** date with the calendar icon.
3. Use the pencil icons to check or edit **Ship To**, **Invoice To**, and **Sales Rep** (name, email, phone).
4. Click **Place Order** and choose to download a **PDF** or send it by **email** from xtraCHEF.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Vendor and sales rep contact details live in the **Vendors** hub.
- The article states no roles or plan tier.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- You can only order items that already exist as invoice items, meaning things you've been invoiced for before. A brand-new item has to come through on an invoice first.
- The article describes no EDI or punch-out integration. Orders go out as PDF or email only.
- Prices come from your invoice history, so they're estimates, not quotes.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Build order guides per station (kitchen dry goods, bar, brewery ingredients) so staff order from approved lists in fewer clicks.
- Keep rep emails current in the Vendors hub so email orders reach the right person. Email orders also give you the vendor's **Confirm Order** status.
- For fixed weekly orders, use a scheduled order guide with **Auto Submission** (see Order Guides) instead of creating orders by hand.
