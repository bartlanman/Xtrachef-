## What it is
A **COGS group** is a reporting bucket that ties a Toast sales category to one or more purchase categories, for example Beer, Food, or NA Beverage. Defaults are provided. You can rename them, deactivate them, flag them as having no sales, or add your own. There's no standard setup, because every operation is different.

## When you'd use it
- During initial COGS setup, to match the groups to how you think about the P&L.
- When you add a revenue line, such as merch or events.

## How to do it
**Edit**
1. Go to **gear icon** (upper right) **> COGS groups**.
2. Click a row. The View and Edit buttons stay greyed out until a row is selected.
3. Click **Edit COGS Group**. In the pop-up, change the name, set it **active/inactive**, or mark that the group **won't contain sales** for reporting.
4. Click **Save**. The change applies immediately.

**Add**
1. On COGS groups, click **Add COGS Group** (upper right).
2. Fill in the same fields (they start blank) and click **Save**.

Then map the groups in COGS Mapping. Sales categories come from Toast Web. Purchase categories are created in xtraCHEF or synced from accounting.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Fields: name, active/inactive, and a no-sales flag. No roles are stated.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Use the **no-sales** flag for cost-only groups (for example, To-Go Supplies or Paper) so their COGS % isn't computed against zero sales.
- Make a group inactive instead of deleting it, to keep history.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Line the groups up with your QBO COGS accounts (Food COGS, Beer COGS, and so on) so xtraCHEF COGS % ties to the P&L.
- Basic City idea: separate **House Beer** (brewing ingredients and packaging) from **Guest/Resale Beverage**. House-brewed beer COGS behaves very differently from resale.
