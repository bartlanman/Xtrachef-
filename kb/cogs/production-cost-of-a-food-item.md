## What it is
This is a short FAQ. It asks where to enter what a menu item costs to make so you can track profit per item. **Toast Web has no COGS or cost field on menu items.** For restaurants, item costs and COGS live in xtraCHEF (recipes, inventory, and the COGS feature). Retail shops can use **Toast Retail** instead, which stores inventory cost, tracks margins, and can price items to a target margin.

## When you'd use it
- When someone asks "where do I put the plate cost in Toast?" The answer is: not in Toast POS. Use xtraCHEF recipes.

## How to do it
1. Build a recipe in xtraCHEF for the menu item. Ingredient costs come from invoice-linked products.
2. Map the Toast menu item to the recipe so sales line up with cost.
3. Use the **COGS report** for category-level cost and the **Product Mix / Variance** reports for item-level margin.
4. Merch and retail goods: consider Toast Retail's cost and margin fields.

## Settings, options & permissions
- No roles are listed. Recipe costing needs the right xtraCHEF tier. The Pro tier is required for food prime cost per the upload article; *verify for your plan*.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Plate costs entered anywhere other than xtraCHEF recipes won't feed COGS or Product Mix reports.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Recipes that pull live invoice prices recalculate plate cost automatically as vendor prices move. Review Product Mix monthly for items whose margin has slipped.
- For Basic City: beer-by-the-glass and cans can be costed as recipes, using kegs and packaging as products, so draft margins appear next to food.
