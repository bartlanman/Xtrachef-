## What it is
An onboarding roadmap for groups with several locations. It's sequenced, but many steps can run in parallel. The Onboarding Consultant (OC) is the main contact. It also covers how tenants, COA and vendor lists work across locations.

## When you'd use it
- Onboarding more than one location, such as a taproom and restaurant set up as separate locations, or a second site.
- Deciding whether locations share a tenant.

## How to do it
1. **Connect accounting**: QBO or QBD guides. This is required for AP automation and advanced reporting, but **not** for Recipes or Inventory. Not on QuickBooks? Send your COA to the OC to upload.
2. **Configure the account**: Sales Setup, COGS group mapping, optional custom categories, and products (Pro).
3. **Attend the First Steps webinar** (live or on demand): invoice uploads and GL mapping.
4. **Upload invoices**: at least **10 from different vendors**, ideally **20+**. Use desktop, mobile or EDI.
5. **Attend the Item Review webinar** (Pro: Recipe/Inventory). Review **50+ items** before meeting the OC.
6. **1:1 check-in** with the OC. They send a scheduling link after the item review webinar.
7. **Keep reviewing items** in Item Review.
8. **1:1 Recipe & Inventory training** once **50–100 items** are verified: recipes, prep recipes, Toast menu mapping, count lists. Ask the OC to copy recipe and inventory work across locations.
9. **Finish onboarding**. Keep uploading invoices **at least weekly**. Ask the OC to add any new locations.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Location adds go through the OC and a Tenant Admin.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **One tenant = one COA list + one vendor list** for all its locations, even if several QBO companies are connected. Those QBO companies need **identical COA and vendor lists**. Otherwise, make them match or get separate tenants.
- Work **can be copied between locations within a tenant, but not between tenants**. Separate tenants mean repeating the work.
- No on-site training, and no "done-for-you" setup.
- Setup takes from a few weeks to about 90 days.
- xtraCHEF does **not** track inventory consumption in real time. Depletion is shown after the fact, based on counts.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- For Basic City: if the brewery, taproom and restaurant all use one QBO company, keep them in one tenant as separate locations. Use QBO class or location tracking instead of separate tenants so recipes and count lists can be copied.
- Build recipes and count lists at one location first, then ask the OC to copy them.
