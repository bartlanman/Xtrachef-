## What it is
The official onboarding roadmap: four Essentials steps, then eight Pro-only steps. Each step lists its objectives and homework. Your Onboarding Consultant (OC) schedules the training and calls. The steps run in order, but many can overlap.

## When you'd use it
- To plan the rollout, know what to finish before each call, and track progress.

## How to do it
**Essentials**
1. **Self onboarding** (15–30 min): activate the account, review billing, confirm locations, add users, start the accounting integration (QBO or QBD), map COGS groups, upload **10+ documents**, and schedule webinars.
2. **Kickoff call** (30 min Zoom): process and timeline, accounting details, webinar scheduling, follow-up calls, and **checking which vendors are EDI-eligible**. Homework: upload **20+ documents** and attend **First Steps**.
3. **Activation & education** (self-guided): keep uploading, watch **Action Needed** invoices, reconcile through **Map Invoice Items**, add optional custom categories, map purchases and sales to **COGS groups**, and finish **Sales Setup** (needed for the JE sync).
4. **Wrap-up or Pro handoff** (30 min): create users, upload, clear Action Needed, map items, and **extract invoices to accounting** (Extract Monitor). Essentials ends here. Pro customers move to a new Pro consultant.

**Pro only**
1. **Preparation**: attend the Item Library webinar, then create **10–15 products**.
2. **Pro kickoff** (30 min): agree on recipe and inventory goals and review the Item Library. Homework: Inventory Management webinar and any Item Library fixes.
3. **Inventory training** (up to 60 min): build **Inventory Areas & Count Lists** and complete one count.
4. **Inventory follow-up** (30 min): review areas, lists and counts, and go over the **COGS report**.
5. **Recipe training** (up to 60 min): build recipes and prep recipes and map them to menu items and modifiers. Homework: **20+ recipes** built and mapped.
6. **Recipe follow-up** (30 min): recipe reporting.
7. **Health check-in** (30 min): recipe reporting and Inventory Analytics.
8. **Wrap-up**: ongoing support options.

## Settings, options & permissions
- A Tenant Admin runs most of the setup steps.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **Multiple QBO companies in one tenant** need **identical COA and vendor lists**. Otherwise, make them match or use separate tenants.
- No on-site training or done-for-you setup. Timeline is a few weeks to 90 days.
- Inventory isn't tracked in real time. Work can't be copied between tenants.
- Ask the OC to copy recipe and inventory work to other locations, and to add new locations.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Treat each call's homework as a checklist with due dates on a calendar.
- Bring the EDI vendor list (PFG, Sysco, Fintech beer and wine) to the kickoff call to get feeds started early.
- For Basic City's Pro recipes, prioritize core drafts, top food sellers and cocktails to reach the 20-recipe target.
