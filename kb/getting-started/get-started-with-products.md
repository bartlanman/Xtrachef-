## What it is
**Products** are your ingredients and inventory items. Each product groups one or more invoice line items that are substitutes for each other, such as the same item from two vendors or a vendor substitution. Products are the foundation for Recipes and Inventory (Pro).

## When you'd use it
- Before building recipes or count lists.
- When a vendor substitutes an item or you switch vendors. Attach the new line item to the existing product so costs carry over.

## How to do it
1. Create products (see "Create Products").
2. Attach invoice line items to products. A new vendor SKU gets a new item number and description that xtraCHEF may not link to the existing product automatically, so add it to the product manually.
3. **See where a product is used**: **Item library > View products**, then the **three dots > Product details** on the product's row. This shows its linked invoice items, recipes and count lists.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Item Library and catalog management are available to TA, EX and RM.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **Be specific**: "Onion, White" and "Onion, Red" are separate products.
- **Name it type first, then the difference**, so it sorts alphabetically: "Milk, 2%", "Milk, Whole".
- **Alcohol includes the brand**: "Beer, Heineken", "Vodka, Tito's", "Draught Beer, Guinness".
- **No duplicates**: one product per ingredient.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- For Basic City, keep naming consistent: "Keg, [Brewery] [Beer] 1/6", "Malt, Pilsner 2-Row" (if brewing inputs are tracked), "Cheese, Cheddar Shredded".
- Each month, check vendor substitutions against products. An unlinked substitute leaves recipe costs at the old price.
