## What it is
How to add xtraCHEF to a Toast account, or add xtraCHEF to another location, by requesting a demo in the Toast Shop. A sales rep then handles enrollment and onboarding. The article also lists pricing for each tier.

## When you'd use it
- Buying xtraCHEF, adding a location, or upgrading from Essentials to Pro.

## How to do it
1. Log in to **Toast Web**.
2. Pick the location in the **location drop-down** at the top left. It only appears if you have several locations.
3. Click **Shop** in the left nav.
4. Go to **Software solutions > Costs and inventory**, or search for "xtraCHEF" with the magnifying glass.
5. Pick a tier:
   - **Essentials: $179/month/location**. Invoice automation and accounting sync, plus food cost reporting, price tracker, vendors and orders.
   - **Pro: $279/month/location**. Everything in Essentials plus **Recipe Costing** and **Inventory Management**.
6. Click **Get a Demo**. A sales rep will contact you.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Needs Toast Web access to Shop for that location.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Pricing is per location. A second location doubles the cost.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Pro costs $100/month/location more. That's worth it if recipe costing and AvT find more than about $100/month in pour or plate variance, which is usually easy on a draft program.
