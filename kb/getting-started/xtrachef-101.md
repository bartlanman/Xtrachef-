## What it is
An overview of what xtraCHEF does and how its two tiers differ. xtraCHEF uses AI to read supplier invoices line by line, pulls sales from Toast, and produces near-real-time cost and margin reporting. The Pro tier adds recipes and inventory.

## When you'd use it
- To decide between Essentials and Pro.
- To explain the system to a new manager or bookkeeper.
- To plan the order of setup work.

## Tier comparison
| Feature | Essentials (formerly "Lite") | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice automation | Yes | Yes |
| Accounting sync | Yes | Yes |
| Class & location tracking (QBO) | Yes | Yes |
| Food cost reporting | Yes | Yes |
| Ingredient price tracker | Yes | Yes |
| Vendor & order management | Yes | Yes |
| Recipe costing | No | Yes |
| Inventory management | No | Yes |

Pro adds plate costs and recipe costing, inventory counts, and advanced analytics on margin, profit and inventory depletion (waste, theft, breakage, shrinkage).

## How to do it (recommended order)
1. **Onboarding & configuration**: work with the Onboarding Consultant. Connect accounting, sync Toast Web and Toast Payroll, and upload invoices.
2. **Products (Pro)**: create products (ingredients) one at a time or by import. This is required before recipes or inventory.
3. **Recipes (Pro)**: build recipes and connect them to Toast menu items through Product Mix Mapping to track margin and prime cost.
4. **Inventory counts (Pro)**: build count lists from products and complete counts to power Inventory Analytics.
5. **Data & insights**: food cost, COGS and spend reports populate automatically. Pro adds recipe costing, waste and shrinkage reporting.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Tier controls which features you can use. Recipe and Inventory menus only appear on Pro.
- US only.
- To sign up, request a demo through Toast Shop.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Recipes and inventory depend on Products, so the products work has to come first.
- Reporting quality depends on regular invoice uploads and complete mapping.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- For a brewpub, Pro's value is concentrated in plate and pour costing (cost per pint from keg invoices, cost per burger) and AvT variance on the bar. Essentials covers AP automation and the QBO sync.
- The Price Tracker (both tiers) can serve as a weekly vendor price-creep alert. Pair it with a PFG-vs-Sysco comparison.
- Toast Classroom offers live "First Steps" sessions, a fast way to train a new manager.
