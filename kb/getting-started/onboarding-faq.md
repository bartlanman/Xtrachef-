## What it is
Common questions about xtraCHEF onboarding: timing, number of calls, who attends, logins, EDI and locked modules.

## When you'd use it
- Planning or troubleshooting onboarding.

## Key answers
- **Do I need to be live on Toast POS?** No. A few invoices are enough to start.
- **Onboarding Consultant calls**: **Essentials = 2**, **Pro = 9**. More are possible if you and the OC agree they're needed.
- **Duration**: Essentials is about **1 week**. Pro is about **7 weeks** to get inventory and recipe costing running.
- **OC calls may be flagged as spam.** The OC also emails and texts.
- **Can't log in?** Contact your OC first, then live chat (Mon–Fri, 9:00–5:30 ET, through Toast Web, Payroll or Toast Now).
- **No invoices yet?** Tell the OC so they can adjust the plan.
- **Skipped self-onboarding?** You can finish it on the kickoff call, but it's better done beforehand.
- **Who attends**: the account point of contact plus any stakeholders (owner, GM, bar manager, accountant).
- **Accountant**: helpful if you're syncing accounting. Otherwise, provide QBO login details. Non-QBO users bring an Excel file of GL codes and vendors.
- **QBO users don't need a COA file** if they provide QBO credentials.
- **Automated invoice uploads (EDI)** are available with **Sysco, Ben E. Keith, Fintech, Gordon Food Service, Performance Food Group (PFG), US Foods, DiCarlo**. Have account numbers and rep contacts ready. Invoice data can update within about an hour.
- **Lock icon on Inventory or Recipe** = you're on Essentials. Upgrade to Pro through your rep or OC.
- **Recipes**: the OC builds **at least 5** recipes with you after the Recipe webinar.
- **Inventory**: the OC builds **at least 1** count list with you after the Inventory webinar.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- The OC doesn't build everything. You do the bulk of the setup.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- For Basic City: set up **PFG and Sysco EDI** first (both are on the supported list), plus a **Fintech** EDI feed if beer and wine distributors bill through Fintech (common for VA alcohol distributors). These three likely cover most invoice volume with no uploads.
- Use all 9 Pro calls deliberately. Bring a prepared list of recipes (draft pours, top food items) and count-list areas.
