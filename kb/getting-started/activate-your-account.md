## What it is
Activation is the self-onboarding module that turns on a new xtraCHEF account. It covers the password, terms of service, goals, users, accounting software choice, sales-category-to-COGS pairing and first invoice uploads. Toast strongly recommends finishing it before the first meeting with your Onboarding Consultant.

## When you'd use it
- Right after purchase, when the activation email arrives.
- When a user can't log in to the mobile app. The account may not have been activated from the email.

## How to do it
Have these ready first: the legal business name, address and phone; admin access to your accounting software; and some invoice files (PNG, JPG, JPEG or PDF).
1. **Get started**: open the email from **no-reply@toasttab.com** and click **Let's get started**.
2. **Activate your Toast account**: set a password of at least 7 characters with at least one letter and one number, then click **Activate**. You'll be logged out. Log back in with the new password and click **Continue**.
3. **Terms of Service**: read the terms, tick the box at the bottom left, and click **I Accept**.
4. **Share your goals**: answer the welcome questions about your priorities, then click **Let's do it!**
5. **Add users**: you're listed as the **Account Owner**. Click **+Add user** for anyone else. You can also do this later. Required fields (red asterisk): **First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Role, Location**.
6. **Select accounting software**: choose **QuickBooks Online**, **QuickBooks Desktop**, **Other / I don't know** or **None**.
7. **Pair sales categories with COGS groups**: click **COGS**, then match each Toast sales category to an xtraCHEF COGS group. Several sales categories can share one COGS group. You can change this later.
8. **Upload invoices**: upload recent invoices so reporting starts and the AI learns your vendors' layouts. This step is optional but highly recommended.
9. Finish the module. Next, attend training, add more users and keep uploading.

## Settings, options & permissions
- The person who activates becomes the **Account Owner**.
- A user's **Role** controls which modules and functions they can use. See User Roles.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- The **mobile app login won't work** until the account has been activated from the email.
- Uploading **10+ invoices per location** "unlocks" your data and reporting.
- Accepted file types are PNG, JPG, JPEG and PDF.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- For Basic City, a sensible COGS pairing during activation: Toast sales categories for Draft Beer, Cans/Crowlers and Kegs to go → **Beer** COGS group; Food → **Food**; Wine/Cider/Spirits → **Liquor/Wine**; Merch → **Retail/Merch**; NA beverages → **NA Bev**. Check this against the actual Toast sales categories.
- Add the bookkeeper and kitchen manager during step 5 so the right roles are in place from day one.
- Have a batch of the last 2–4 weeks of invoices from every major vendor ready to upload in step 8. Starting the AI on varied invoices speeds up learning.
