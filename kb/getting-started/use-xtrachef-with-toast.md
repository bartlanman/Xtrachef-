## What it is
A short introduction to xtraCHEF as Toast's accounts-payable automation product. It digitizes and archives invoices, extracts line-item detail, and reports spend trends, ingredient price changes, food cost and margin. xtraCHEF Pro adds full food-cost management analytics.

## When you'd use it
- To orient a new user.
- To confirm who sets up the Toast connection.

## How to do it
1. **Enable the integration**: Toast Customer Care connects your Toast Web account to xtraCHEF. To confirm it's done, ask your Onboarding Consultant or email support@xtrachef.com.
2. **Learn the product**: use the expert-led training sessions in Toast Classroom ("Getting Started with Toast xtraCHEF") or the Support Center articles.
3. To talk to sales, use the **Talk to Rep** button.

## Settings, options & permissions
- US only. Not available in Australia, Canada, Ireland or the UK.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- You can't link Toast and xtraCHEF yourself. Toast does it.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Ask Toast to confirm the link and the date sales data starts. That date is the earliest business date the sales JE sync can cover, so it matters for cutover planning (for example, a Shogo → xtraCHEF switch).
