## What it is
The ongoing routines that keep xtraCHEF data, and therefore its reports, accurate after onboarding. The guidance is split into Essentials-and-Pro tasks and Pro-only tasks.

## When you'd use it
- As the basis for daily, weekly and monthly xtraCHEF routines.

## How to do it
**All tiers (Essentials + Pro)**
1. **Keep uploading and reconciling invoices** (desktop or mobile) so spend and vendor prices stay current.
2. **Monitor accounting extracts**:
   - Clear **Action Needed** invoices (exceptions) and map new invoice items in **Map Invoice Items**.
   - Check the **Extract Monitor** (invoices → accounting) and **Sync Monitor** (sales JEs) and fix any failure reasons.
3. Use the **Analytics** tab, **Reporting** tab and **COGS report** to monitor performance.

**Pro only**
4. **Review new items in the Item Library**. New or substituted invoice items must be attached to products, or recipe and inventory costs go stale. Check unreviewed or unapproved items regularly.
5. **Create and map recipes for new menu items**. New Toast items sync over with no recipe and no cost until mapped.
6. **Keep inventory count lists current**. Add new dishes and ingredients.
7. Use the **Product Mix Report** and **Inventory Analytics** to diagnose problem areas.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Extract Monitor is TA/RM. Sync Monitor is available to all roles except Invoice Capture. Item Library is TA/EX/RM.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Reports are only as good as the data behind them. Stale uploads mean wrong COGS and food cost.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- **Daily**: clear Action Needed, map new items, glance at Sync Monitor to confirm yesterday's sales JE posted.
- **Weekly**: Extract Monitor failures, Item Library unreviewed items, new Toast menu items to map, Price Tracker review.
- **Monthly**: inventory count, COGS report, AvT/PMIX review, then close the accounting period.
- An agent can run the weekly checklist read-only in the browser and report counts: open exceptions, failed extracts, unmapped items, unmapped menu items.
