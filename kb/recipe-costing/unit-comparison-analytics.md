## What it is
A report that puts two or more locations (or concepts) side by side and compares their spend in an expense grouping against each location's total sales. It's filed under Recipes & Costing, but it's really a multi-unit spend benchmark.

## When you'd use it
- When there is more than one location or concept in the tenant, such as the taproom/restaurant and a second venue or event space set up as its own xtraCHEF location.
- To check whether one location's food cost, packaging or to-go spend is out of line with the others.

## How to do it
1. Go to **Reports > Unit Comparison**. Click **Ok** to close the pop-up.
2. Choose an **Expense Group** at the top: **Category**, **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) Group**, or **General Ledger Code (GL)**.
3. In the top bar, select **two or more Locations**.
4. Pick a **date range** (up to **3 months**) and click **Refresh**.
5. Read the side-by-side view of each location's expense group against its total sales.

## Settings, options & permissions
- No roles are listed in the article.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- You need at least 2 locations.
- The date range can't be longer than 3 months.
- Nothing populates until you click **Refresh**.
- The comparison is only as good as your coding. Make sure the Categories, COGS groups and GL codes are mapped the same way at every location.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- If Basic City runs separate xtraCHEF locations (for example the brewery production side and the taproom/kitchen), compare them by COGS Group each quarter, in three 3-month windows.
- Single-location tenants get little from this report. Use Spending by Category or GL analytics instead.
