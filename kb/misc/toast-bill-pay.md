## What it is
**Toast Bill Pay** is a vendor-payment tool built on xtraCHEF. You pay supplier invoices by **ACH or check**, either now or on a scheduled date, in full or in part, and you can apply credits. Completed payments sync automatically to **QuickBooks Online**. It's free for xtraCHEF + Toast customers. **However, it isn't currently available to new subscribers.**

## When you'd use it
- Paying vendors straight from the invoices xtraCHEF has already processed, without retyping them into QBO or a bank portal. This applies only if your account already has access.

## How to do it
1. **Accept the terms for each location**: log in at app.sa.toasttab.com, go to **Bill Pay > Start Now**, check the box, and click **I agree**. An active **deposit bank account in Toast Web** is required. All tenant admins at that location get a confirmation email.
2. **Payment methods**: you land on the Bill Pay settings page. The Toast Web bank account, already verified during Toast onboarding, is pulled in automatically.
3. **Map GL accounts (QBO)**: on the same page, go to **Sync to accounting system** and map each payment method to its QBO bank account so payments post with the right funding source.
4. **Vendor remit details**: go to **Vendors > [vendor] > Bill Pay** and add the payment preference (ACH or check), bank and remit details, remit address, and a notification email for payment confirmations. You can also do this while paying a bill.
5. Pay bills (see "xtraCHEF: Pay Vendor Bills With Bill Pay"). The dashboard shows completed and upcoming payments, filterable by status.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Only the **Tenant Administrator** role has access.
- You need both Toast and xtraCHEF. **xtraCHEF-only customers are excluded.**
- Support: support@xtrachef.com.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- **Not open to new subscribers right now.** Check whether Basic City's account has it before planning around it.
- Terms have to be accepted **per location**.
- **ACH reaches vendors in 5 to 6 business days**, so schedule with that lead time. Check timing isn't stated.
- No fees are listed other than "free".

## Automation / efficiency angles
- If you have it: schedule payments against due dates (payment terms) to hold cash as long as possible without paying late. Payments sync to QBO automatically, so no manual bill-payment entry is needed.
- If you don't: the parallel workflow is xtraCHEF extracting bills to QBO and paying through QBO Bill Pay or the bank.
