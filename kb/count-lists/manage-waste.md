## What it is
A waste log. It records the quantity and dollar value of stock lost to burnt food, spoilage, breakage, a burst keg and so on. Logged waste appears as **Wastage** in Actual vs Theoretical and Depleting Inventory, so known losses are separated from unexplained variance.

## When you'd use it
- Any time stock is thrown out: spoiled produce, a dropped tray, a dumped or blown keg, a dumped batch of a prep recipe, bad cans.
- To backfill waste from an earlier day before running AvT.

## How to do it
1. Go to **Inventory > Manage Waste**.
2. Click **+ Add Waste**.
3. (Optional) Pick an earlier date from the calendar drop-down.
4. Find the item with **Search** or narrow by **Type**. Best practice is to log by **Product** so analytics stay accurate.
5. Choose a **Reason** and enter a quantity.
6. (Optional) Click the **three dots** next to **Wastage Total** to add notes or a photo.
7. Add more items in the same session if needed.
8. Click **Submit** (top right). The day's completed waste log is displayed.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Roles aren't stated. See Access to Inventory Features by Role.
- The article doesn't list the Reason options. Check them in the live account.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Log waste against the **Product** (not a single invoice item) so it lines up with recipes and counts.
- Waste only reduces unexplained variance if it's logged inside the AvT date window.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- **Brewery use:** log blown or foamed-out kegs, line-cleaning loss, and product dumped at a keg change as waste on the keg product (in gallons). That keeps draft variance meaningful.
- Use the photo and notes for comps and breakage to create a paper trail. Have the closing manager do a nightly waste entry from a back-of-house waste sheet.
- An agent can compare weekly waste totals by Reason and flag spikes (for example, spoilage of one produce item points to over-ordering, so lower its PAR).
