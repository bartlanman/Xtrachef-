## What it is
The **Invoices** tab in the xtraCHEF mobile app shows where every uploaded invoice and receipt is in reconciliation for a date range. You can filter by Processed, Pending, or Rejected.

## When you'd use it
- To confirm that a delivery invoice you just photographed actually went through.
- For a quick daily check from the floor without opening a laptop.

## How to do it
1. Install the xtraCHEF app (iOS or Android) and log in.
2. Tap **Invoices**, next to the camera icon at the bottom center.
3. The **Invoice Reconciliation** tab opens by default and shows uploads in the selected date range with their stage.
4. At the top, filter by status:
   - **Processed**: uploaded and processed successfully.
   - **Pending**: xtraCHEF is still reviewing it. Not yet ready for reconciliation.
   - **Rejected**: xtraCHEF rejected it. See Invoice Rejections for the reasons.
5. Handle anything that needs input using the Action Needed workflow (the **Tasks** tile).

## Settings, options & permissions
- No specific roles are stated. You need a mobile device and an app login.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Pending is normal for a short time after upload. Something stuck in Pending for a long time is worth a support ticket.
- This view doesn't fix anything. Fixes happen in Tasks, or on desktop for unbalanced invoices.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Receiving routine: photograph the invoice, then check the Rejected filter before the end of the shift.
- Combine with push notifications so rejections come to you instead of you having to look.
