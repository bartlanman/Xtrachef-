## What it is
Assigned approvers can work the invoice approval queue in the xtraCHEF mobile app. Approval rules (configured separately on desktop) hold flagged invoices out of reconciliation until someone acts. The app supports four actions: Reject, Change To, Assign To, and Approve.

## When you'd use it
- An owner or GM approving high-dollar or unusual invoices from anywhere.
- Clearing an approval backlog that is holding up AP or extracts.

## How to do it
1. Install the app (iOS or Android) and log in.
2. Tap **More** in the bottom nav bar, then the **Approvals** tab. You'll see every invoice waiting on you.
3. Tap the arrow next to an invoice name to see the scanned image.
4. Choose an action:
   - **Reject**: tap Reject, enter a **reject reason** (required), add notes if you want, then tap **Submit**.
   - **Change To**: switches the document between Invoice and Receipt. Confirm in the **Confirm Change** pop-up.
   - **Assign To**: hands the invoice to another user in the account. Pick the user and confirm.
   - **Approve**: reconciliation resumes and a success message appears.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Only users **assigned as approvers** see invoices in this queue.
- Approval rules are created on desktop (see the Approvals article).

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- Approval rules are designed to pause invoices that would otherwise auto-reconcile once ML learning is mature. Unapproved invoices sit idle and never reach costing or accounting.
- A rejected invoice needs a reason, so be specific to help whoever re-uploads it.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Pair this with mobile push notifications for approvals so the queue never stalls.
- Use **Assign To** to route invoices to the department lead who placed the order. For example, kitchen invoices go to the chef and brewing supplies go to the head brewer.
- Keep rules tight, such as amount thresholds or new vendors, so only exceptions reach the approver and routine invoices flow through.
