## What it is
The xtraCHEF app can send push notifications for invoice events that need someone's attention: approvals, Action Needed invoices, unmapped invoice items, and extraction failures. Each user picks which types they want. The same notifications also appear on the xtraCHEF website.

## When you'd use it
- To keep AP moving without someone having to log in and check.
- To route alerts by role. For example, an approver turns on approvals and a bookkeeper turns on extract failures.

## How to do it
**Enable**
1. The first time you log in to the app, choose **Allow Notifications** at the "Turn on notifications?" prompt.
2. If you chose **Not now**, turn on push for xtraCHEF later in your phone's notification settings.

**Customize or disable**
1. In the app, tap the **Account** tab (bottom right).
2. Under **Settings**, tap **Notifications**.
3. Toggle each notification type on or off.

## Settings, options & permissions
- Settings are per user. The article doesn't name role restrictions.
- Categories covered: approvals, invoices needing action, unmapped items, and extract failures.

## Rules, limits & gotchas
- If push is blocked at the OS level, the in-app toggles do nothing. Check the phone's settings first.
- The article doesn't list individual toggle names or version requirements.

## Automation / efficiency angles
- Give each alert type an owner: GM for approvals, kitchen manager for unmapped items, bookkeeper for extract failures. That way nothing falls through and nobody gets alert fatigue.
- Extract-failure alerts are the early warning that QuickBooks Online is missing bills, so keep them on for whoever owns the books.
